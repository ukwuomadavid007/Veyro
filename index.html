Standard Pushup

Target Muscles Chest, Shoulders, Core
Difficulty Beginner / Intermediate
Energy Rate ~8.5 kcal/min

Correct Execution & Form

Place hands slightly wider than shoulder-width. Lower until chest nearly grazes the ground, pushing vigorously upward while preserving a rigid hollow-body plank.

✓ Key Focus Points

  • Elbows tucked at ~45-degree arrow angle
  • Squeeze glutes and brace abdominal wall

✗ Common Pitfalls

  • Flaring elbows out to 90 degrees
  • Allowing lower back to hyperextend/sag

Breathing Rhythm

🌬 Inhale deeply during eccentric descent • Exhale sharply on concentric press
